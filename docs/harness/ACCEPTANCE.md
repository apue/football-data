# Acceptance Criteria

- A single goal receives at most one goal-context bonus per goal event.
- An official assister receives contextual credit for the goal they assisted.
- On 2026-07-15, Messi ranks above Lautaro on the Player of the Day surface while Lautaro remains the higher Impact Pick candidate.
- On 2026-07-14, Rodri is surfaced as a Player of the Day challenger and ranks above Oyarzabal on the whole-match surface.
- Selection-review payloads require a Player of the Day verdict referencing a selected player and an explicit challenger when one exists.
- A one-match day recommends one public card and permits at most two.
- Existing match-flow, editorial, SQL smoke, and full pytest suites pass.
- v0.4 and bounded loop v1 remain available as rollback configurations.
